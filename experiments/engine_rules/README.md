# Engine rules: do-not-contact, timing, holdout (tm-models)

**ALL NUMBERS HERE ARE SYNTHETIC** (sandbox stub accounts plus simulated labels, not T-Mobile customer data).
By David's rule, the word "synthetic" never appears in any value these modules emit (columns, why_line text,
notes); it lives only here and in code comments. A test checks this, and the engine's own `reason`/`data_note`
labels are stripped from `offers_with_rules.csv`.

These are separate modules that post-process the engine's per-account output and join on `account_id`.
`harness/offers.py` (owned by tm-offers) is not edited.

## The three rules in plain words

**1. Do-not-contact (`guardrail_rule.py`).** Each account gets the first reason that applies; `contact_ok` is True
only when no reason applies. `why_line` is one plain sentence per account (at most 140 characters, built only from
bill, plan and support fields).
- `bill_change_no_fix`: the bill went up in the last 3 billed months for a real reason. Text only a credit that offsets
  the increase. **New rule:** an increase that comes only from a new phone the customer chose doesn't block anything,
  and a fix-only callback is always allowed. That leaves 562 of the 1,412 bill-change accounts able to block.
- `receipt_only_plan_move`: cheaper-plan moves never go out as texts. They go on the bill or receipt only.
- `contacted_this_quarter`: at most one proactive contact per 90 days. This is a hook: put `account_id,last_proactive_contact_date`
  in `proactive_contacts.csv`. The data has no outreach log, so today it blocks nobody.
- `not_worth_contacting`: the account isn't picked by the policy. That policy is retention-only, picked at a 10% true
  save rate, with each kept account worth $1,478 (the engine's $1,835 × 19.338/24). With `--policy engine`, the
  engine's own picks are audited instead.

On the shipped `demo_data`, the counts match the tm-models scratch run row for row (same contact_ok, reason and why_line
for all 5,042 accounts): **184 OK, 4,041 not worth contacting, 499 receipt-only, 318 blocked by a bill change.**
The policy picks 202 accounts with EV $4,142. The 184 contacted carry $4,109 of it.

**2. Timing (`timing_rule.py`, `events.py`, `rollforward.py`).** The default is **act now**. An account waits only if
a real dated change (here, almost always the care credit's 6-month tenure minimum) makes an offer eligible within
12 weeks, and that offer, discounted for the churn that happens before then, beats the best offer today.
`timing` is `now`, `wait` or `silent`. `wait_until` is the date of the planning week the offer unlocks.
On the 5,000 scored accounts the result is **866 now, 11 wait, 4,123 silent**, the same as the Moment Replay run
account for account. Nine accounts wait until 2026-11-05 and two until 2026-12-03 (9 care credit + fix-first,
1 care credit, 1 family upgrade). The 42 persona accounts have no out-of-fold churn score, so they get `silent`;
for those, `text_send` still follows contact_ok.
- This rule replaces tm-offers' `harness/moment_replay.py` on timing. That module writes 0 waits because it never
  lets a credit be a wait target.
- Allowing "wait" is worth only about +$277 on $68.7k (+0.4%, 95% CI $95–545).
- `data/weekly_candidates_12w.csv.gz` is generated by `rollforward.py`. It re-runs the shipped engine's
  `evaluate_account` week by week on rolled-forward real fields, and the regeneration was checked to match exactly
  (62,474 rows). Timing decisions use the out-of-fold LR churn score and the kept-account value from the moments run
  (the engine's flat basis). `data/upgrade_oof_lr16.csv` is the upgrade score from that run.
- Caveat: 7 of the 184 contact_ok accounts are told to wait. Each would get the fix-first callback now, or the same
  callback plus a $25 credit after a tenure unlock in Nov/Dec. A human would fix the issue now and add the credit later.
  The one-contact rule rules that out today, so a scheduled follow-up credit is a v2 product call.

**3. Holdout (`holdout_rule.py`).** Among contact_ok accounts, a deterministic random 20% is held out:
sha256("2026:" + account_id) gives a number from 0 to 1, and the account is held out if it's below 0.20.
- Held-out accounts get **no text**. Lift = churn of treated contact_ok accounts minus churn of held-out ones.
- Membership depends only on the account id, so it stays the same across reruns.
- Sizing comes from proofloop/RESULTS.md, which tested 5/10/20/50%; the default 20% is the largest non-50/50 share
  tested. On 5k accounts no split can prove a 20% churn cut (power about 16–19%), so this is a launch design.
  At 500k accounts a 20% holdout detects about a 5.9% relative cut.
- Demo: 30 of 184 held out, so **154 texts**: 148 now, 5 on 2026-11-05 and 1 on 2026-12-03.

## Output columns (joined onto offers.csv by account_id)
`contact_ok, block_reason, why_line, timing, wait_until, holdout, holdout_note, text_send`. `text_send` is `now`,
an ISO date for a wait, or empty when there's no text (not contact_ok, or held out).
`guardrail_detail.csv` adds the decision fields, such as bill-change reason/$, policy action/EV, receipt move and
timing action.

## How to run (from the repo root)
```
DUBHACKS_DB=vendor/dubhacks/data/sandbox.db python3 -m experiments.engine_rules.apply_rules            # demo_data
# on a fresh tm-offers v2 engine run (value already $1,478 and save rates field-scaled in a20b2dd+):
TM_OUT_DIR=/tmp/eng DUBHACKS_DB=vendor/dubhacks/data/sandbox.db python3 harness/offers.py
DUBHACKS_DB=vendor/dubhacks/data/sandbox.db python3 -m experiments.engine_rules.apply_rules \
    --engine-dir /tmp/eng --policy engine --save-rate-scale 1 --value-scale 1 --tag v2engine
python3 -m pytest -q experiments/engine_rules/tests      # 9 tests, ~3 s
```
Output goes to `out/<tag>/`: `guardrail.csv` (account_id + the 8 columns), `guardrail_detail.csv`, `summary.json`, and
`offers_with_rules.csv` (git-ignored, regenerable). The sandbox is always opened read-only.

## Hook for tm-offers (they add it, we don't edit offers.py)
In `harness/offers.py` `main()`, right after `cands.to_csv(OUT_DIR / "offer_candidates.csv", index=False)`:
```python
from experiments.engine_rules.apply_rules import run as engine_rules_run
ow, rules, _ = engine_rules_run(engine_dir=OUT_DIR, policy="engine", save_rate_scale=1.0, value_scale=1.0)
ow.to_csv(OUT_DIR / "offers_with_rules.csv", index=False)
rules[["account_id", "contact_ok", "block_reason", "why_line", "timing", "wait_until", "holdout", "holdout_note",
       "text_send"]].to_csv(OUT_DIR / "guardrail.csv", index=False)
rules.to_csv(OUT_DIR / "guardrail_detail.csv", index=False)
```
Without touching offers.py, `harness/guardrail_join.py` can point at these files with
`TM_GUARDRAIL_DIR=experiments/engine_rules/out/<tag>`. The file names and `blocking_bill_change` match what it reads.

## Reconciliation with tm-offers' engine
- **8141024 (gated, 10% save rate): 487 contacts.**
  - 157 are forced port-out (`pick_override=port_out`, all EV ≤ 0) and 330 are normal. By the signal
    `port_out_inquiry==1`, the split is 196 vs 291.
  - Our 184 are a strict subset of the 487. Of them, 32 have a port-out inquiry, but none of those 32 is a forced
    pick: each passed our positive-EV policy on its own. Our policy has no forced-contact rule, so the 157 forced ones
    are out because their EV is ≤ 0.
  - Our guardrail on their 487 picks: **464 pass, 23 blocked by `bill_change_no_fix`** (15 normal, 8 port-out;
    actions: 12 check-in, 11 perk reminder). No account had a positive-EV offsetting credit to swap to.
- **a20b2dd committed (open ticket forces fix-first): 1,735 contacts.**
  - Our guardrail passes 1,732 and blocks 3 check-ins. Fix-only is always allowed, so all 1,514 forced open-ticket
    callbacks pass. 1,508 of the engine's own picks have EV ≤ 0.
- **a20b2dd + tm-offers' uncommitted working tree (fix-first forced only when its EV > 0; port-out as a separate
  "already leaving" line): 423 contacts.**
  - Contacts are 252 normal, 163 port-out save attempts and 8 fix-only.
  - Our guardrail: **412 pass, 11 blocked by `bill_change_no_fix`** (all check-ins: 5 normal, 6 port-out).
  - Excluding the 163 port-out save attempts (all with engine EV ≤ 0, and all on the already-leaving line), **255 pass**.
  - 161 of our 184 are contacted. The other 23 (21 perk reminders, 2 fix-first) are silent in the engine as
    `open_ticket_ev_le0`, because an open ticket allows only fix-first messages.
