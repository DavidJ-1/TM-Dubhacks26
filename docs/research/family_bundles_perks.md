# T-Mobile family / household / bundle / perk offers → sandbox mapping
_Research only, compiled 2026-10-17 (all URLs accessed 2026-10-17 PT). Real public offers are cited for context; **all customer data and every number in our product are synthetic**. Customer texts may only quote $ amounts that exist in sandbox `promotions` / `plans` / `addons`._

Sandbox: `vendor/dubhacks/data/sandbox.db`, opened with `file:...?mode=ro` (read-only, nothing written). metadata: `brand = "T-Mobile (stub data, not official)"`, `as_of = 2026-10-01`, `schema_version = 9`, `seed = 2026`.

Legend: **Dir** = brief direction (#3 Next Best Moment retention, #5 Household Mix Optimizer, plus #1 Cart / #2 Matchmaker / #4 Timekeeper where relevant). Confidence: ✅ official T-Mobile page, current · ⚠️ official but internally inconsistent or possibly stale · 🟡 third-party / anecdotal.

## Headline findings
1. **The sandbox mirrors real 2026 list prices closely.** Plan `line_prices` equal T-Mobile's Broadband-Facts pre-AutoPay prices (Essentials 4 lines $140, More 4 lines $220, Beyond 4 lines $280, More 55+ 2 lines $110, Essentials 4-Line Offer $30/line). Home Internet $50/$60/$70 with $15 off with a voice line matches Rely/Amplified/All-In. Beyond's $5 connected-device line matches the real "watch & tablet lines $5/mo". ⚠️ The sandbox sets `prices_include_autopay=1`, but the real numbers that match are *before* AutoPay. This doesn't affect the logic, but don't claim "with AutoPay" in copy.
2. **Kid/teen lines already exist in the sandbox.** `lines.member_role` takes the values `child` (1,544 lines) and `teen` (1,538 lines) across **1,526 accounts**, with labels like "Kid", "Teen A" and "Younger child". the offer engine's data-gap note ("no kid-age fields") is only half true: we can get `kid_line` / `teen_line` flags directly from sandbox data, with no simulated enrichment needed. Age *bands* would still be simulated.
3. **Two concrete Household-Mix wins exist in the data today:**
   - **66 Experience Beyond accounts pay for a $10 or $15 Smartwatch Line** (`ADD-LINE-WATCH[-PLUS]`) when `ADD-LINE-CONNECTED-BEYOND` costs $5 (real T-Mobile benefit too). Savings are $5–10/watch/mo, it's a pure customer win, and the "do the right thing" story fits Customer value at 30% of the rubric.
   - **188 accounts have 4+ lines on standard Essentials** ($65+35+20+20 = $140 for 4) where `PLAN-ESSENTIALS-4LINE` costs $30×4 = $120, saving $20/mo at 4 lines. Real T-Mobile runs the same 4-Line Offer (limited time).
4. **Family-ready offers the sandbox lacks:** 3rd Line Free, SyncUP KIDS watch plan, FamilyMode / Family Allowances, Netflix/Hulu/Apple TV as named perks (the sandbox only has generic `streaming-video-*` perk codes), T-Mobile Tuesdays / Magenta Status, and the reported retention credits ($10×6 / $20×12). These can appear in the strategy doc and as *non-$ copy* ("included with your plan"), but **no $ amount from them can go in a customer text**.
5. **Retention credits are not officially published.** The only sources are Reddit-derived press reports. The sandbox's `PROMO-STUB-LOYALTY-CREDIT` ($5/mo, tenure ≥36) and `PROMO-STUB-CARE-CREDIT` ($25 one-time, ≥3 contacts in 90d) are `source='stub'`, invented by the sandbox. Label them that way.

## Mapping notes for the offer engine (proposals, not code)
- **Family upgrade (kid reaching phone age)**: signal is `member_role='child'` on an account with no matching phone, or a watch line plus age band (simulated). Action: add a line at the plan's next `line_prices` slot, optionally with `PROMO-STUB-FREE-SELECT` ($300.99/device, prime credit, new line) or `PROMO-STUB-IPHONE17E` ($700 over 36 mo, new line + port-in, so ⚠️ port-in makes it ineligible for existing-account adds). The lock-in to model is the device-credit `payout_months` (24–36), which is how real T-Mobile "locks in" (credits stop if you leave). There's no contract term.
- **Younger kid (5–11)**: smartwatch line instead of a phone: `ADD-LINE-WATCH` / `ADD-LINE-CONNECTED-BEYOND` + `PROMO-STUB-WATCH-LINE`. This mirrors the real SyncUP KIDS positioning ("pre-smartphone").
- **Bill-shock price lock**: no "price lock" promo SKU. Use a plan migration to a SKU with `price_guarantee_years=5`, and say "5-year price guarantee" in copy (no $). For Essentials households with ≥4 lines, `PLAN-ESSENTIALS-4LINE` is a real $ saving that the sandbox can quote.
- **Household optimizer quick wins**: Beyond + $10/15 watch → $5 connected line (66 accts); Essentials ≥4 lines → 4-Line Offer (188 accts); no HINT + high usage → HINT bundle $15/mo off; 55+ couples on standard More/Beyond → 55+ SKUs.
- **Do nothing / $0 actions**: perk reminders (Netflix/Hulu/Apple TV already included, Tuesdays). These cost about $0 and are honest, but they have **no sandbox SKU**. Treat as "check-in" actions with zero cost and copy that names no dollar amount.
- **Don't use for retention**: `FAMILY-PAYOFF`, `BYOD-CARD`, `KEEP-YOUR-PHONE` and most device credits require `requires_port_in=1`, so they're for new customers. That's realistic: real Keep & Switch / Family Freedom became new-accounts-only in July 2026.

## Uncertainties / stale flags
- Apple TV: "$3/month" vs "included at no additional cost" on the same official page. Experience More upgrade cadence: 2 vs 3 years across official pages.
- HINT bundle discount: $15 (plans table) vs "$20/month" (deals hub, probably a limited-time promo).
- SyncUP KIDS plan price: $12/mo (2024 newsroom) and $5/mo with Beyond (aggregator) are not confirmed on a live T-Mobile page because pricing loads via JS.
- The standalone smartwatch line price wasn't captured (JS). Sandbox $10/$15 is plausible but unverified.
- Family Allowances: "free on Magenta 2+ lines" (legacy marketing page) vs "small monthly fee" (support). Probably free with FamilyMode ($10).
- Retention credits come only from Reddit-sourced press. Never present them as official T-Mobile policy.
- Magenta Status perks rotate (2024 launch article). Tuesdays perks change weekly.
- Device/tablet promo codes on the promo-details page date from 2025 to Mar 2026 and may have rotated.
- The sandbox flags `prices_include_autopay=1`, but its numbers match real *pre-AutoPay* list prices.
- Not looked up: T-Mobile Fiber bundles, Protection<360> family pricing, prepaid/Metro, international add-ons.
